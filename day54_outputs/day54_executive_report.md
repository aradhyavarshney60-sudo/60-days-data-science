# Day 54: Executive Customer Churn Insights

## Executive Summary

The dataset contains **7,043 customers**, including **1,869 churned customers**. The observed churn rate is **26.54%**.

## Key Business Metrics

- Total customers: 7,043
- Churned customers: 1,869
- Retained customers: 5,174
- Churn rate: 26.54%
- Average monthly charges: $64.76
- Average tenure: 32.4 months

## Highest-Churn Segments

- **Contract:** Month-to-month (42.71% churn)
- **InternetService:** Fiber optic (41.89% churn)
- **PaymentMethod:** Electronic check (45.29% churn)
- **TechSupport:** No (41.64% churn)
- **OnlineSecurity:** No (41.77% churn)

## Recommended Business Actions

1. Prioritize retention analysis for Month-to-month customers, which has the highest observed contract-level churn rate (42.71%).
2. Study retention practices among Two year customers, which has the lowest observed contract-level churn rate (2.83%).
3. Review early-tenure customer journeys and onboarding to identify opportunities to improve customer retention.
4. Test targeted retention interventions and evaluate their impact before scaling them across customer segments.

## Interpretation Note

These results describe observed associations in the supplied dataset. They do not establish causation or guarantee future customer behavior. Validate proposed interventions with testing.
