# Day 54: Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize retention analysis for Month-to-month customers, which has the highest observed contract-level churn rate (42.71%).
2. Study retention practices among Two year customers, which has the lowest observed contract-level churn rate (2.83%).
3. Review early-tenure customer journeys and onboarding to identify opportunities to improve customer retention.
4. Test targeted retention interventions and evaluate their impact before scaling them across customer segments.
