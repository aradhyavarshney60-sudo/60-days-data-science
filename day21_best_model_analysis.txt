Important Model Selection Trade-offs:
- A model with high accuracy may still have poor recall.
- Low recall can be a serious issue in fraud detection because fraudulent transactions may be missed.
- High precision is useful when false positives are costly.
- F1 Score provides a balance between precision and recall.
- ROC-AUC helps evaluate the model's ability to distinguish between classes.
- Interpretability and business requirements should also be considered before deployment.

Fraud Detection Recommendation:
For fraud detection, Recall and Precision are especially important because both missed fraud and false alarms can have business consequences.
Therefore, the final production model should be selected using business requirements along with F1 Score, Recall, Precision and ROC-AUC.
